## Shipped gameplay promise and acquisition

This mod is a **notice presentation and transport library**, not a source of collectible items or blocks. Feature mods can call `GameplayNotices.send(ServerPlayer, GameplayNotice)` to send a server-owned, themed title/hint to one client (`GameplayNotices.java`, `NoticeNetwork.java`). The client presents a FIFO discovery queue, suppresses simultaneous duplicates by notice identity, plays a page-turn sound on first visible frame, and clears its ephemeral queue on logout (`NoticeQueue.java`, `NoticeClient.java`). The five themes are THREADS, COMBAT, TRACES, SPAWN and LIFE (`NoticeTheme.java`, `NoticeStyle.java`); their display times are 8 s, 3.2 s, 3.2 s, 8 s and 8 s respectively. Client-only callers may submit a single keyed, validity-checked transient notice; discovery arrivals supersede it, and an unseen transient expires after five monotonic seconds. `NoticeReminder` retries an unseen prompt after five seconds and schedules its next attempt one minute after actual presentation. Hidden HUD/screens pause render progression, but the client tick still maintains transient validity/expiry.

**Acquisition / recipe-root / GUI references:** this repository registers no item, block, recipe, creative-tab entry or container/menu. Its player-facing surface is the HUD overlay in `NoticeClient.render`; neither a recipe-root nor an inventory acquisition path exists here. Test the actual triggering item/block acquisition, recipes and source-mod GUI in each **calling** feature mod and the assembled pack, not by inventing a notice-mod recipe. For this library, check visibility, overlap, scaling and dismissal in a real client, including screens/HUD-hidden behavior and both themed and transient notices.
